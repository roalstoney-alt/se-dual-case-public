# SE-PHYSICAL-AI-001 · From Humanoid Demonstration to Bounded BMW Deployment

**Subject:** Figure AI; Figure 02 and Figure 03, BMW Group manufacturing.  
**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-10-21 (date precision).  
**Public-projection review state:** LOCAL_PROVISIONAL_v0.1; human review required.  
**Question:** What does the public record establish about deployment of a humanoid robot in manufacturing, and where does generalization outrun the evidence?

## Supported as attributed disclosures

- Figure AI and BMW described a bounded Figure 02 sheet-metal loading/positioning task in BMW Spartanburg production. Their publicly reported cumulative program figures include **over 90,000 parts**, around **1,250 hours** of operation, and contribution to production of **over 30,000 BMW X3 vehicles**. These are source-party disclosures, not an independently audited fleet telemetry series. [F-S01, F-S02, F-S03]
- Figure described an **84-second total cycle** and **37-second load** as task *requirements*, plus **greater than 99% placement success** and **zero operator interventions per shift** as *targets*. Neither a target nor a requirement is an achieved measured distribution. [F-S01]
- BMW and Figure announced a **Figure 03 logistics/sequencing demonstration** at Spartanburg in June 2026. That new robot generation and task must be evaluated separately; Figure 02's result does not establish Figure 03's sustained performance or production acceptance. [F-S03, F-S04]
- Figure separately reported manufacturing output and first-pass-yield indicators for Figure 03, and a financing round. These are manufacturer-origin claims, not independent tests of field durability or cost competitiveness. [F-S05, F-S06]

## Stop-point · what cannot yet be established

Reviewed sources do **not establish** achieved placement-rate distributions, intervention frequencies, per-robot life/MTBF/MTTR, long-term safety acceptance and operating economics, broad general-purpose industrial readiness, or repeatable accepted production performance for Figure 03's distinct logistics workflow.

**Do not infer:** 1,250 cumulative operating hours per robot; the >99% target as realized success; or a proof of Figure 03 maturity inherited from Figure 02. Lack of accessible public evidence is **not** proof that these metrics do not exist.

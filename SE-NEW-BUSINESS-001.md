# SE-NEW-BUSINESS-001 · IREN's Bitcoin Mining to AI Cloud Transition

**Subject:** IREN Limited; developing AI Cloud Services as a new business line.  
**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-10-21 (date precision).  
**Public-projection review state:** LOCAL_PROVISIONAL_v0.1; human review required.  
**Question:** Which milestones are evidenced by dated issuer filings and disclosures, and where are contracts, delivery, capacity, earned revenue and economic returns still distinct?

## Attributed evidence chain

- **2023:** IREN disclosed NVIDIA H100 procurement intentions and customer discussions. A purchase order or commercial discussion does not itself establish active GPU service delivery or revenue. [I-S01]
- **2024:** IREN disclosed an initial **248-H100** Poolside service agreement and a later expansion to **504 H100s** with time-limited terms. Contract announcements do not establish full execution or durable profits. [I-S02, I-S03]
- **FY2024–FY2026:** IREN's FY2026 filing reports AI Cloud Services revenue of **USD 3.105 million**, **USD 16.394 million** and **USD 128.795 million**, respectively. These are attributed historic financial-statement figures, not ARR forecasts, cash proceeds, or proof of segment profitability. [I-S04]
- **2025-11:** IREN disclosed a Microsoft GPU-services agreement with an approximately **USD 9.7 billion headline contract value**, staged Horizon deliveries and contractual contingencies. Contracted consideration is not revenue already recognized or money collected. [I-S05]
- **2026-08:** IREN stated Horizon 1 was delivered to and accepted by Microsoft. This is **IREN-reported customer acceptance**; a separate Microsoft-authored acceptance document was not captured in this review. It does not establish acceptance of Horizons 2–4. [I-S08, I-S09]
- **Transition/economics:** IREN discussed mining asset decommissioning, capacity reassignment, impairment, financing, AI-service expansion and company-defined contracted versus operating ARR. These terms refer to different time periods, measurement bases and operational stages. [I-S04, I-S06, I-S07, I-S09]

## Stop-point · what cannot yet be established

The reviewed disclosures support a **reported transition into GPU cloud services, historical reported AI Cloud revenue, disclosed contracts, and IREN's statement of Horizon 1 acceptance**.

They do **not** establish a separate Microsoft-signed acceptance record, completion or acceptance of Horizon 2–4, full collection of the contract headline value, completion of the overall mining-to-AI transition by the evidence cutoff, or sustainable economic returns after operating costs, financing and capital investment.

**Do not equate** contracted ARR with operating ARR, either ARR measure with GAAP revenue, agreement MW with commissioned/accepted IT load, or impairment with equivalent cash expenditure.
